Classic Mango Pomelo Sago 杨枝甘露

Crowd Favourite! Made with freshly procured mangoes, smooth ice-blended slush with grapefruit bits and refreshing coconut milk. The combination of fresh and creamy flavors creates a delightful contrast. The slush captures the essence of fruity and milky goodness, delivering a rich, satisfying taste that remains light and refreshing.

Super Strawberry Daifuku 超嗲草莓大福

Indulge in the exquisite Super Strawberry Daifuku! This delightful drink features premium strawberries paired with a delicate Jasmine Needle King tea base, offering a perfect blend of rich aroma and refreshing taste. The sweet and tangy strawberry chunks, silky milk pudding, and chewy rice mochi at the bottom create a symphony of textures and flavors, delivering a multi-layered taste sensation with every sip.

White Orchid Biluochun Fresh Milk Tea 东方白兰

White Orchid Biluochun Fresh Milk Tea features Biluochun tea, one of China's top ten famous green teas with a history spanning over a thousand years. This exquisite tea is blended with white orchid flowers, creating a rich and aromatic experience. Combined with smooth, refreshing milk, it offers a delightful and refreshing taste, evoking the essence of spring.Enjoy each of the light and refreshing sips.
